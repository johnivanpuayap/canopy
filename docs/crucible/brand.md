# Canopy — Brand Identity

## Name

Canopy

The layer of a forest that shelters everything growing beneath it — the app is the living layer over all your projects and ideas. Works as a folder name, CLI reference, and app name.

## Tagline

All your projects, one living view

## Tone of Voice

- Formal ←→ Casual: **Leans casual** — friendly, plain language, no corporate speak
- Playful ←→ Serious: **Balanced** — light touches, but the content is your real work
- Technical ←→ Approachable: **Approachable** — built for developers, but never jargon-first
- Minimal ←→ Expressive: **Minimal** — quiet interface, the projects are the color

## Personality

- **Calm** — a quiet place to see everything at once; no noise, no urgency theater
- **Organized** — structure without ceremony; things have a place
- **Encouraging** — progress is made visible; the app celebrates motion, not guilt
- **Feel:** Like opening a clean notebook in a quiet park

## Logo

Logo system saved in `logos/` directory:

- **Primary logo** (`logo.svg`) — combination mark: an organic tree-canopy symbol beside the Canopy wordmark
- **App icon** (`logo-icon.svg`) — the canopy symbol alone, square composition
- **Wordmark** (`logo-wordmark.svg`) — "Canopy" set in Outfit with tuned letter-spacing
- **Favicon** (`favicon.svg`) — simplified canopy shape, legible at 16px

Each variant includes full color, monochrome dark, and monochrome light versions.
See `logos/USAGE.md` for clear space rules, minimum sizes, and implementation guidelines.
